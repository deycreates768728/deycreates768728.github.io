Contaminated Phase II RCT: Antihypertensive Efficacy Inferencia 2026

Analysis of a simulated Phase II RCT (180 obs., 13 variables) testing drug "Trx" against placebo for systolic blood pressure reduction across 3 sites, navigating a mid-trial device switch and protocol deviations. Adjusted linear regression confirmed a highly significant SBP reduction (p < 0.001); missingness was diagnosed as MAR via logistic regression and resolved with truncated-normal imputation. Site-specific analysis used Bonferroni correction, and a sensitivity analysis excluding the compromised site showed only 2.69% variance in the treatment effect. Co-authored with Pratik Majumdar and Sampad Nag, Dept. of Statistics, Presidency University.

Survival Analysis of the MGUS2 Cohort

A full R survival-analysis pipeline — Kaplan-Meier estimation, multivariable Cox proportional hazards, competing risks (Aalen-Johansen CIF), multi-state modeling, AFT models, MICE imputation, and landmark analysis — on a 1,384-patient cohort. Found a double dissociation in prognostic factors: age/sex/creatinine predicted mortality while M-spike size alone predicted progression. Diagnosed a proportional-hazards violation via Schoenfeld residuals and validated Cox results against an independently fitted Weibull AFT model. Delivered as a formal academic report with full mathematical notation.
